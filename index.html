About Me

I am a System Integration Engineer at Infostellar in Tokyo, where I manage development of the StellarStation ground station as a service platform. My work spans technical project management for a cutting-edge optical space communications system, onboarding multi-million dollar satellite missions, and building browser-based dashboard tools for mission operations. Before Infostellar, I spent three and a half years as a Flight Software Engineer at Astroscale, working on the ELSA-d and ADRAS-J spacecraft — developing multi-sensor navigation software for on-orbit docking, running hardware-in-the-loop tests, and supporting live mission operations.

My path into aerospace started a little differently: after earning a BA in Political Economics from the University of Washington, I ran a startup building interactive electronic escape-room puzzles that was later acquired by FLEE Escape Games. I then completed a BS in Computer Science at Oregon State University, with internships at Spectranetix building software-defined radio demos and at NASA researching a service provider model for the Space Mobile Network. I speak English natively and Japanese conversationally.
