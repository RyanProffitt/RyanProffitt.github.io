Live Satellite

A continuously running satellite simulation operated in real time. Uses the NASA Core Flight System framework. Every satellite instance I “launch” is treated as if it is real. That means real engineering design phases from concept to shipment. It also means realistic operations such as waiting for ground station contacts, driving modes, proper communication protocols, end of mission upon loss of spacecraft, etc.

All mission data is available via the main Grafana dashboard. Grafana dashboards for particular missions of interest are also made available.

I hope to build out a satellite design that I can use later for a real satellite when launch costs come down in several years.

AI Assisted Spacecraft Operations

A tool and procedure to use AI to assist with mission operations (LLMs, agentic AI, etc). There are two identified use cases within the scope of this project:

  1. Passive monitoring and suggestion of changes in real time for standard operations.
  2. Active assistance with operational activity at the time of request from a user.

The first use case involves the tool using the spacecraft design documentation, concept of operations, the past and current states of the spacecraft, and mission events as input. The output is a one-off change proposal that includes what the change is, the command sequence and conditionals to proceed through the sequence, the reasoning for the change, and a description of the intended state the spacecraft will be in at the end of the change.

Hosted Satellite Architecture

A framework for a satellite software architecture that allows for hosting of customer mission subarchitectures safely. In other words, with this framework I could launch my own satellite with which I could rent out time and functionality to others. A potential use case is to make a bit of extra money once the primary mission is over by renting out time on the satellite. Customers may want to test their logic with a real satellite in orbit (for example trying out a particular piece of hardware, testing their proprietary attitude control logic, to validate operations, or to train team members).
